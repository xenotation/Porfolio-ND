  • Assemblages and agency

    A concise introduction to Deleuze & Guattari’s assemblage (agencement): heterogeneous couplings of people, objects, and signs; machine vs. enunciative assemblages; coding, stratification, and (re/de)territorialization, with examples from literature, bureaucracy, and schooling.
    Art Salon and Concert Studio of Radio Varna, Varna
